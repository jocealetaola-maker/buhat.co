buhat Café

Everything behind the counter, in one app.

Built inside a working café in Davao and used there every day. It runs on the tablet at the counter and on your phone. Nothing to install.

Point of sale

Fast ordering, best sellers up top, cash, GCash or split payments. GCash payments get checked by the person who holds the account.

Shifts & staff logins

Each barista signs in with their own PIN and clocks in and out. Every sale and expense is tied to the shift that made it.

Inventory & recipe costs

Recipes deduct stock as you sell. See what each drink costs to make, and get a heads-up before you run out.

Expenses with approvals

Staff log what they spend from the register. The owner approves it. Receipts and partner cash advances are all on record.

Payroll & payslips

A weekly calendar from real clock-ins: late, overtime and holidays worked out for you. Print payslips and certificates of employment.

Reports that answer questions

Sales by hour, day, week or month, compared with before. Margins, best sellers and a business health check.
